## Desktop apps

Ando is available for macOS Intel, macOS Apple Silicon, and Windows. Download the desktop app at [download.ando.so](https://download.ando.so). Most features match the web app, but you get native OS notifications, always-on access with dock alerts for unread messages, and keyboard shortcuts that won't conflict with your browser presets or custom shortcuts.

### Configure macOS notifications

After you install Ando on your Mac:

1. Open **System Settings**.
2. Click **Notifications**.
3. Select **Ando** from the app list.
4. Turn on **Allow Notifications**.
5. Choose **Banners** or **Alerts**.
6. Turn on the notification options you want, such as **Sounds**, **Badges**, and **Show previews**.

If Ando does not appear in the Notifications list, open the Ando desktop app once, allow notifications when macOS prompts you, then return to **System Settings** -> **Notifications**.
