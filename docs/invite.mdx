Use Invite when you are ready to bring teammates into your Ando workspace.

## Invite a teammate

1. Open Ando.
2. Click **Invite** in the sidebar.
3. Enter the teammate's email address.
4. Choose the workspace role, if Ando asks for one.
5. Send the invite.

The teammate receives an email with a link to join your workspace.

## Who can invite

Workspace owners and admins can invite new members. Some workspaces also allow members to invite teammates, depending on workspace settings.

If you do not see Invite, ask a workspace owner or admin to send the invite or update your permissions.

## After someone joins

Add new teammates to the channels they need, point them to important pinned conversations, and ask them to open Inbox so they can see mentions and followed threads.
